Wednesday 11 November 2026. Russia and Ukraine held their first direct talks in nine months in Abu Dhabi, US-mediated; after more than four hours the first day ended without a breakthrough and talks continued into a second day, even as drone attacks went on (BBC). In the US midterms, Democrats secured the House, 254 seats to the Republicans' 163, and at least 54 Senate seats, with 16 races still uncalled a week after polling (AP). The US charged three men as Iranian operatives over plots against Jewish communities and RAF Fairford in Britain (Department of Justice), and France blamed Iran's Revolutionary Guards for hacking some 700 supermarket refrigeration sites, spoiling food (AFP). Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has moved to a military installation after Iranian threats, and travels to China next week arguing to "de-risk, not decouple" (New York Times). A reported tanker incident in the Strait of Hormuz lifted Brent crude to about $92 a barrel. US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, with core prices up 0.3%, both above forecasts; the 10-year Treasury yield neared 5% while the S&P 500 slipped 0.38% (BLS). World leaders opened the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, with energy security high on the agenda (AFP). Anthropic filed to go public (SEC), TSMC reported "insatiable" chip demand (Nikkei), and Nvidia fell 5.5% amid reports that its chief executive faces a federal investigation. India cut the prices of 137 cancer medicines by up to 70% (NPPA). A fog pile-up on a Bulgarian motorway killed 12 people, and a bus crash in Seongnam, South Korea, injured 57. After nearly 50 years in space, Voyager 1 is closing on one light-day from Earth (New Scientist).
